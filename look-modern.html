About the salon

A Christchurch salon for hair extensions

As the name says, hair extensions are at the heart of Beyonce Hair Extensions. It's a Christchurch hair salon on Papanui Road, and the easiest way to find out what's possible for your hair is to get in touch with the salon.

Planning your visit

What should I ask before my appointment?

A short chat before you book helps you arrive knowing what to expect. Here are some useful things to ask when you phone or email the salon.

  1. The look you'd like

    Mention the length, volume or colour you'd like. A reference photo sent by email can make this much easier to describe.

  2. The price

    Prices aren't listed on this page, so ask for a quote for the result you have in mind.

  3. How long it takes

    Ask how long your appointment is likely to take so you can plan the rest of your day.

  4. Aftercare

    Ask how to look after your extensions at home and when you might need to come back.

  5. Opening hours

    Check which days and times the salon is open before you make the trip to Papanui Road.
